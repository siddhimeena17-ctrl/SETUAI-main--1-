# SetuAI Product Context

## What It Is

SetuAI is an active AI literacy initiative convened by Summit Intelligent
Systems, Shikivaa Foundation, and SKYPA Foundation to build practical,
responsible AI learning with schools and communities.

## Current Truth

SetuAI is operating as an active initiative and continues to refine its public
information, partner process, and learning materials with a careful, evidence-
based approach. The website should reflect current work clearly while keeping
public claims precise and grounded in what has been reviewed.

## Visitors

- School and education nonprofit leaders
- Sponsors and companies
- Educators, reviewers, and future volunteers
- Families and community partners

## Design Register

Clear, caring, rigorous, and materially grounded. Use sharp geometry,
structured whitespace, accessible contrast, decisive imagery, and purposeful
motion. Avoid inflated impact claims, pill-heavy components, generic card
grids, and decorative motion that obscures content.

## Product Principles

1. State what is known, what is actively being built, and what still needs review.
2. Keep inquiries, newsletter consent, and sponsorship interest separate.
3. Make all public content editable through the protected custom admin.
4. Prefer one reviewed source of truth over competing CMS fallbacks.
5. Protect young people and personal data before promoting a program.
6. Keep Hindi content in editorial review until it is complete; a partial
   machine-translated experience is not a public language launch.
7. Treat the AI assistant as unavailable until gateway activation has been
   verified in the deployed environment.
